# Workshop and Library

This chapter explains the workspaces for research and source materials, how they connect to your writing, and how to protect your work.

- **Notes** holds independent writing and personal ideas.
- **Books** organizes writing into structured pages.
- **Library** is the workspace for original materials and existing managed resources.
- **Workshop** brings together Run, Templates, Outputs, Chat, Usage, AI Settings and Translation Settings. Availability is shown on each page; **Coming soon** means that the action is not enabled.

Original materials and generated drafts have separate roles. Original files belong to Library; generated Markdown belongs to Workshop Outputs. Writing in Notes and Books remains editable. Use the following pages to switch workspaces, import originals and browse resources and understand backup and recovery.

See [Selection Translation](translation.md) for optional English-to-Chinese assistance across the workspaces.

See [Token Usage and Model Costs](usage.md) to review local AI consumption.

# Workshop 与 Library

本章介绍研究与原始资料工作区、它们与写作内容的关系，以及保护个人资料的方法。

- **Notes** 用于独立写作和个人思考。
- **Books** 将写作组织为结构化页面。
- **Library** 用于原始资料和现有受管理资源。
- **Workshop** 包含 Run、Templates、Outputs、Chat、Usage、AI Settings 和 Translation Settings。各页面会显示操作是否可用，**Coming soon** 表示尚未启用。

原始资料与生成稿分别承担不同职责：原始文件归属 Library，生成的 Markdown 归属 Workshop Outputs。Notes 和 Books 中的写作内容保持可编辑。后续页面说明工作区切换、原文导入与资源浏览，以及备份与恢复。

全工作区可开关的英译中辅助见 [Selection Translation](translation.md)。

用量与费用统计见 [Token Usage and Model Costs](usage.md)。
