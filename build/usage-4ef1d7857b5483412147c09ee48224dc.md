# Usage, Costs and Balances

## Usage records and filters

Open **Workshop → Usage**. **Today**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** and the From/To dates control the summary and request list. Filter by source, model or purpose to inspect a particular service or activity.

The summary shows **Requests**, **Tokens** and **Costs**. The **Requests** table lists individual calls, newest first, with total tokens and cost; it shows ten rows per page. A missing token count or charge is unknown, not automatically zero. Tests are also requests and can appear here.

## Activity heatmap

**Hourly** shows a selected day in 24 squares arranged in three rows. **Daily** shows the selected year's calendar. These activity periods are separate from the summary's From/To dates. Hover over a square for its time or date, token usage and cost. Darker or stronger colors show higher activity, not a different price tier.

## Model prices and currency

In **Model prices**, enter the prices published by the service for the exact model and currency. Prices are per **one million tokens**. Use the input/output and supported cache-price fields where applicable. For a model that is free on your account, enter zero for its applicable token prices.

When a service returns a charge, LanCarbon uses it. Otherwise it estimates from returned usage and the price settings recorded for that request. The estimate is not a live copy of the provider's billing website. Editing a model's price does not retroactively replace the price snapshot of a completed request. Removing a model and its associated price settings does not erase saved historical charges.

Set the **USD-to-CNY exchange rate** for approximate conversion to RMB in the overview. This is a manually maintained display value, not a live exchange-rate feed or the bank's settlement rate.

## Estimated account balances

Choose **Set balance** or **Update balance**. Enter an account name, currency and the full balance currently shown by the provider. LanCarbon then deducts the reported or estimated costs of later recorded requests. It does not fetch or change the real provider balance.

**Update balance** opens with the estimated current balance filled in. After a top-up or when you want to correct the estimate, replace that number with the provider's new full balance. Charges incurred outside LanCarbon are not automatically known, so occasional manual calibration is useful.

The low-balance reminder uses the account's currency: below **¥5** for CNY or **US$2** for USD. The threshold does not depend on the interface language. Use **Unlimited · No balance tracking** when you do not need balance reminders, for example for an account used only with free models.

## Connections sharing an account balance

Under **Connections sharing this balance**, select the Chat, translation or other connections charged to the same provider account. Different API keys can share one balance; different accounts on the same platform should remain separate. Each connection belongs to one balance account at a time.

For example, a SiliconFlow Chat key and a separate SiliconFlow translation key can share one tracked balance when both bill the same account. If you change that grouping, enter the full current account balance again instead of adding old card balances together.

## Differences from provider billing

Usage is stored locally and updates from requests made through LanCarbon; viewing saved records needs no separate billing lookup. Estimates may differ because of missing usage, promotions, tiered prices, exchange rates or other activity on the account. Use the provider's bill for actual payment and use LanCarbon's view to understand activity and anticipate top-ups.

# 用量、费用与余额

## 用量记录与筛选

打开 **Workshop → Usage**。**Today**、**Last 7 days**、**Last 30 days** 和 From／To 日期控制统计卡片与请求列表。还可以按来源、模型或用途筛选，查看某项服务或活动。

统计卡片显示 **Requests**、**Tokens**、**Costs**。下方 **Requests** 表按时间倒序列出单次请求的总 Token 和费用，每页十条。接口未返回用量或费用时，应理解为未知，不是自动按零计算。测试也是请求，也可能记录在这里。

## 活动热力图

**Hourly** 用三行共 24 个方格显示选定日期的小时活动；**Daily** 显示选定年份的日历。这些活动时段与统计卡片上方的 From／To 日期独立。悬停方格可查看时间或日期、Token 用量与费用。颜色更深或更强表示活动更多，不代表价格档位不同。

## 模型价格与币种

在 **Model prices** 中，按服务商为具体模型公布的价格填写币种和单价，单位为**每百万 tokens**。按实际情况填写输入、输出及支持的缓存价格。账户可免费使用的模型，将适用价格填为零。

平台返回费用时优先使用返回值；否则根据返回的 Token 和该请求保存的价格设置估算。这里不是服务商账单网站的实时副本。修改模型价格不会追溯替换已完成请求的价格快照；删除模型及关联价格设置也不会删除已保存的历史费用。

通过 **USD-to-CNY exchange rate** 设置美元转人民币的近似显示汇率。它由你手动维护，不是实时汇率，也不等于银行实际结算汇率。

## 估算账户余额

点击 **Set balance** 或 **Update balance**，填写账户名称、币种，以及服务商当前显示的完整余额。随后 LanCarbon 从该起点扣减以后记录到的请求费用或估算费用，不会查询或修改平台真实余额。

打开 **Update balance** 时会预填当前估算余额。充值后或需要校准时，将它替换为平台最新的完整余额。在其他软件中产生的费用不会自动进入 LanCarbon，因此可以定期手动校准。

低余额提醒按账户币种判断：人民币低于 **¥5**，美元低于 **US$2**，与界面语言无关。只使用免费模型等无需余额提醒的情形，可选择 **Unlimited · No balance tracking**。

## 共用账户余额的连接

在 **Connections sharing this balance** 中，勾选实际由同一服务商账户扣费的聊天、翻译或其他连接。不同 API Key 可以共享余额，同平台的不同账户则应分开。每个连接同时只属于一个余额账户。

例如，SiliconFlow 聊天密钥和单独的翻译密钥由同一账户扣费时，可以共用一个余额。改变分组后，应重新输入账户当前完整余额，不要把原有余额卡片的数值相加。

## 与平台账单的差异

用量保存在本地，由 LanCarbon 发起的请求更新；查看已有记录不需要额外联网查询账单。缺失用量、活动优惠、分档价格、汇率或账户在其他地方的消费，都可能造成估算差异。实际支付以平台账单为准，LanCarbon 的统计用于了解使用情况和提醒充值。
