## A personal knowledge library, from reading to writing

LanCarbon brings together source materials, reading, AI-assisted understanding, writing and online publishing. You can collect documents, ask questions about them, save what you learn in Notes, and organize longer work as Books. Your knowledge library grows as you add material and develop your own explanations.

You do not need to learn programming. Writing, organizing files and reading documents work locally. AI conversation and translation use a service and model that you configure. Building a website and publishing it are optional activities with their own supporting tools.

![The LanCarbon workspace](assets/2de495e4aed7eca98ea1cfbe93ff676026cc958d049bcddb5065b40f0e00e4d9.png)

The four workspaces in a demonstration project. Screenshots throughout this tutorial use sample content and example settings.

## Find the right workspace

| Workspace | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Notes** | Short writing, reading notes and ideas that can be edited independently. |
| **Books** | Structured chapters and pages; local websites and optional online publication. |
| **Library** | Original documents and writing resources; PDF and EPUB reading. |
| **Workshop** | AI Chat, questions about your sources, translation settings, and usage and cost tracking. |

A useful routine is **collect → read → ask → write → organize**. Publishing is available when you want to share a Book; it is not required to maintain a private knowledge library. Automated paper discovery and collection expansion are not available yet.

## Choose a learning route

### Writing and publishing

Start with [installation and basic concepts](01-getting-started/index.md), then follow Chapters 2–4 to write, build and publish. [Your First Sample Book](05-sample-book/index.md) gives you a complete practice project. You can stop after writing if you do not need a website.

### Reading and building a knowledge library

Read the installation and interface introduction first. Continue to [Library](06-library/index.md) to import and read documents, then [Workshop](07-workshop/index.md) to configure AI and ask questions. Return to Chapter 2 when you want to organize your findings as a Book.

## Keep your work safe

Create your own Notes and Books for exercises. Read [Backing Up and Recovering Your Work](01-getting-started/recovery.md) before moving computers or restoring an older installation. ReadMe describes the current software update; this Book explains the lasting workflow.

# LanCarbon：从 0 到 1

## 从阅读到写作，持续积累个人知识库

LanCarbon 将资料管理、阅读、AI 辅助理解、写作和在线发布结合起来。你可以收集文献，围绕资料提问，把阅读所得保存为 Notes，再用 Books 组织长期积累的内容。随着资料和个人理解不断增加，逐步建立自己的知识库。

你不需要先学习编程。写作、资料管理和文档阅读在本地完成；AI 聊天与翻译使用你自行配置的服务和模型。生成网站与在线发布是可选用途，各自需要相应的配套工具。

![LanCarbon 工作区](assets/2de495e4aed7eca98ea1cfbe93ff676026cc958d049bcddb5065b40f0e00e4d9.png)

示例项目中的四个工作区。本教程截图均使用演示内容与示例配置。

## 选择合适的工作区

| 工作区 | 主要用途 |
| --- | --- |
| **Notes** | 独立编辑短篇内容、阅读笔记与想法。 |
| **Books** | 组织章节和页面，构建本地网站，并按需在线发布。 |
| **Library** | 管理原始文档与写作资源，阅读 PDF 和 EPUB。 |
| **Workshop** | AI Chat、基于资料的问答、翻译配置，以及用量和费用管理。 |

可以按照“**收集 → 阅读 → 提问 → 记录 → 整理**”使用这些功能。需要分享时再发布 Book，维护个人知识库并不要求公开发布。目前尚未提供自动文献发现和资料库自动拓展。

## 选择学习路线

### 写作与发布

从[安装与基本概念](01-getting-started/index.md)开始，按第二至第四章完成写作、本地构建和发布。[第一本示例 Book](05-sample-book/index.md)提供完整练习。不需要网站时，完成写作即可。

### 资料阅读与知识库问答

先阅读安装和界面介绍，再到 [Library](06-library/index.md) 学习导入与阅读文档，然后到 [Workshop](07-workshop/index.md) 配置 AI 并围绕资料提问。需要把阅读成果整理为 Book 时，再参考第二章。

## 保存与备份

练习时请创建自己的 Notes 和 Books，不要编辑系统教程。迁移电脑或恢复旧安装前，先阅读[备份与恢复写作资料](01-getting-started/recovery.md)。ReadMe 介绍本次软件更新，本教程介绍长期有效的使用方法。
