Getting to Know Workshop

Workshop pages¶

Choose Workshop in the main sidebar. Its available pages are:

PagePurpose
ChatHave conversations and ask questions about selected sources.
AI SettingsSave service connections and Chat models; adjust shared response guidelines.
Translation SettingsChoose the model and instructions for English-to-Chinese selection translation.
UsageReview local token records, costs and estimated account balances.

Run, Templates and Outputs are reserved pages and are not available yet. They are not required for Chat or document questions.

Data sent to AI services¶

Ordinary Chat sends the conversation and response guidelines to the selected service. It does not read all of your Notes, Books or Library automatically. Selecting sources adds material from that scope to document questions. Translation sends the selected text to its configured service.

Library originals and your writing remain local. AI use requires access to the configured service and may incur its fees. LanCarbon follows the computer’s network and proxy settings; there is no separate proxy setup in AI Settings.

Saving Chat results in Notes and Books¶

Use Library to read the original, Chat to ask and compare, and Export to Note to keep a conversation as editable writing. You can then organize useful Notes in your own Books. An exported Note is a saved copy, not a live view of the conversation.

认识 Workshop¶

Workshop 页面¶

点击主侧栏的 Workshop，可以使用以下页面：

页面用途
Chat普通聊天，以及基于选定资料的问答。
AI Settings保存服务连接和聊天模型，调整共用的回答规范。
Translation Settings选择英译中划词翻译使用的模型与提示词。
Usage查看本地记录的 Token、费用和估算账户余额。

Run、Templates、Outputs 是尚未开放的预留页面，使用 Chat 和资料问答不需要它们。

发送给 AI 服务的内容¶

普通 Chat 将对话和回答规范发送给选定服务，不会自动读取所有 Notes、Books 和 Library。选择来源后，资料问答会加入该范围的内容。翻译则将选取的文字发送给翻译服务。

Library 原件和写作内容仍保存在本地。AI 功能需要能够访问配置的服务，并可能产生服务商费用。LanCarbon 沿用电脑的网络和代理设置，不需要在 AI Settings 中另设代理。

将聊天结果保存到 Notes 和 Books¶

使用 Library 阅读原文，用 Chat 提问和比较，再通过 Export to Note 把对话保存为可编辑的笔记。之后可以把有价值的 Notes 组织进自己的 Books。导出的 Note 是保存时的副本，不会随后续聊天自动更新。
